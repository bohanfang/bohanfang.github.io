### Syllabus

This course begins with an introduction to the theory on the Delign-Mumform moduli of stable curves. We will emphasize more on the intersection theory of tautological classes. Later we will move to Cohomological Field Theories (CohFTs) and Givental-Teleman's classification.

*  **References:**

    *   Harris-Morrison, *Moduli of Curves*
    *   Arbarello-Cornalba-Griffiths, *Geometry of Algebraic Curves*, vol 2
    *   Zvonkine, *An introduction to moduli spaces of curves and their intersection theory*
